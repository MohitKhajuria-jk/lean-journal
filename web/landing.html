Your journal, kept personal.

Your logs and photos are stored in private cloud storage and separated by journal. Your private key or a linked Firebase email/password sign-in grants access; don't share it. A secure cookie keeps you signed in for 30 days. Signing out removes it from this browser.

The AI adviser sends your question and, only if you opt in, a selected summary of your recent text logs to Google Gemini. Photos are never included. Google may use free-tier inputs and responses to improve its products. AI messages are not stored by this app, but providers may process them under their own policies.

You can export your logs and permanently delete your journal from Settings. This site does not provide medical advice. Email recovery works only after you verify an email while signed in. Offline drafts are optional, stored on your device, and cleared at sign-out.
